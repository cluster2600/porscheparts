For an explicitly assumed **solid aluminium service-temperature study**, the minimum missing dataset is one identified process/heat state with k(T), cp(T), density/reference temperature, directional expansion and its reference temperature/mean-versus-instantaneous definition, E(T), Poisson ratio, and true stress–plastic-strain curves at relevant temperatures, rates and dwells. ## Lead 1: measured thermal behaviour, incomplete numerical access

[Akwaboa et al., Materials 2023, 16, 4920](https://www.mdpi.com/1996-1944/16/14/4920) — DOI [10.3390/ma16144920](https://doi.org/10.3390/ma16144920).

AlSi10Mg was fabricated on a Concept-Laser Mlab-cusing-R under argon: 50/75/100 W, 0.2/0.4/0.6 m/s, 100 µm hatch and 30 µm layers. The as-fabricated material was tested at 100–400 °C; no separate post-build heat treatment is specified in the inspected methods. Build/heat-flow axis association remains unverified. Three samples were tested per temperature using flash diffusivity/reference-sample calorimetry.

Table 3 directly gives density **2.696 ± 0.005 g/cm³** for Al-100-0.2. The conclusion reports conductivity **118.2 → 172.0 W/(m K)** for 100 W/200 mm/s, but this review does not assign exact temperature coordinates from Figure 2. Complete thermal arrays are not openly deposited in the inspected record; its data statement offers them on request. No request, figure extraction or digitization occurred. This is a thermal comparison lead, not elastic/plastic or CP1/HT1 data.

## Lead 2: explicit model inputs, property provenance incomplete

[Wu et al., Crystals 2022, 12, 1309](https://www.mdpi.com/2073-4352/12/9/1309) — DOI [10.3390/cryst12091309](https://doi.org/10.3390/cryst12091309).

Density is 2650 kg/m³ and Poisson ratio 0.33. The experiment uses Space M200 SLM: 400 W, 200 mm/s, 50 µm layers, reported 150 µm scan interval and 80 °C preheat. Subsequent heating/quenching/air-cooling is studied; the indexed text does not establish a complete heat-state/orientation and original measurement provenance for these property tables. It also reports two inconsistent specimen sizes. These are published **model parameters**, not independently acquired matched coupon properties. Expansion definition, yield criterion and plastic hardening curves remain unresolved. No extrapolation beyond the tabulated range is supported here.

## Remaining lifetime and LPBF gaps

Neither inspected lead supplies a matched hot fatigue/TMF law, cyclic hardening/softening and dwell effects, creep/relaxation law, defect/surface sensitivity or statistical life basis. These remain separate acquisition gates. Solid-state service data also leave powder/liquid properties, latent heat, absorptivity and LPBF thermal-history calibration open.

The next material-modelling step should be a **provenance-only assumed-reference table proposal**, reviewed for state, units, expansion definition and missing plastic curves before any separate sensitivity study. Keep the two research states separate and retain the current selected inputs. Do not splice thermal and strength values into a supposed CP1/HT1/EOS recipe or make a constitutive fit from these scalars.
