## Three independent data-pipeline options

- [VTKReader](https://github.com/NVIDIA/physicsnemo/blob/3b8db78c088fe72ddf38e174c36228a6962ea1f7/physicsnemo/datapipes/readers/vtk.py): implemented STL surface loading; the VTP reader and requested VTU volume branch both explicitly raise `NotImplementedError`. Do not infer working field/volume ingestion from the advertised format list.
- [TransolverDataPipe](https://github.com/NVIDIA/physicsnemo/blob/3b8db78c088fe72ddf38e174c36228a6962ea1f7/physicsnemo/datapipes/cae/transolver_datapipe.py): separate reader-backed preprocessing for surface or volume samples, coordinate/SDF/geometry and global-feature options. The example uses prepared Zarr data. Preserve IDs, units and target associations through any conversion; this pipeline is not inherently restricted to one model family.
- [DoMINODataPipe](https://github.com/NVIDIA/physicsnemo/blob/3b8db78c088fe72ddf38e174c36228a6962ea1f7/physicsnemo/datapipes/cae/domino_datapipe.py): reader-backed surface/volume/combined preprocessing, sampling and normalization. Its configuration is independent of the model choice and needs a reviewed 962 data adapter.

A custom volume/graph adapter remains necessary if those schemas do not preserve the accepted solver data. The current [Reader base interface](https://github.com/NVIDIA/physicsnemo/blob/3b8db78c088fe72ddf38e174c36228a6962ea1f7/physicsnemo/datapipes/readers/base.py) separates CPU tensor/metadata loading from device transfer; it is an interface pointer, not a fourth ready-made 962 datapipe.

## Two reference examples

- [Deforming plate README](https://github.com/NVIDIA/physicsnemo/blob/3b8db78c088fe72ddf38e174c36228a6962ea1f7/examples/structural_mechanics/deforming_plate/README.md) and [training source](https://github.com/NVIDIA/physicsnemo/blob/3b8db78c088fe72ddf38e174c36228a6962ea1f7/examples/structural_mechanics/deforming_plate/train.py): **model** HybridMeshGraphNet, **data** example-specific prepared graph/TFRecord trajectory loader, **strategy** single-GPU or data-parallel DDP. This supplies a structural deformation/rollout pattern, not a thermal-stress head model. Its [dataset source](https://github.com/NVIDIA/physicsnemo/blob/3b8db78c088fe72ddf38e174c36228a6962ea1f7/examples/structural_mechanics/deforming_plate/deforming_plate_dataset.py) explicitly assumes one adjacency per simulation and excludes adaptive remeshing.
- [Transformer aerodynamics README](https://github.com/NVIDIA/physicsnemo/blob/3b8db78c088fe72ddf38e174c36228a6962ea1f7/examples/cfd/external_aerodynamics/transformer_models/README.md) and [training source](https://github.com/NVIDIA/physicsnemo/blob/3b8db78c088fe72ddf38e174c36228a6962ea1f7/examples/cfd/external_aerodynamics/transformer_models/src/train.py): **models** Transolver or GeoTransolver, **data** TransolverDataPipe with prepared Zarr surface/volume samples, **strategy** single-GPU or DDP. Useful for geometry/parameter-to-field plumbing; aerodynamic fields and losses do not transfer as accepted head thermomechanics. Optional experimental UQ/PEFT branches are outside this check.

## Required engineering handoff

1. **Dataset admission:** accept reference thermal/structural/process cases first, including convergence, force/energy checks and declared material/process provenance. Retain failures separately. Each immutable sample needs geometry/mesh hashes, connectivity and stable IDs, metal/fluid/interface and BC masks, units/frame/time, load/process history and temperature-dependent material state; label arrays must distinguish node/cell/quadrature location, displacement reference and stress convention. Software coupons and renders are not head labels.
2. **Training contract:** define the operating envelope, target fields and supervised/rollout task, then split complete geometries/builds/load histories before fitting train-only normalization or sampling statistics. Keep related time steps and mesh refinements in the same partition. Pin ETL, interpolation/graph construction, source commit, dependency/runtime/config/seed and checkpoint identities. Compare a simple baseline with multiple families under a separately approved compute budget. The current [package metadata](https://github.com/NVIDIA/physicsnemo/blob/3b8db78c088fe72ddf38e174c36228a6962ea1f7/pyproject.toml) requires Python >=3.11,<3.15; A Python3.10 interpreter would not meet that requirement; fake-provider guard runs do not qualify a training environment.
3. **Held-out validation:** keep an untouched geometry/process/load-case test set. Predeclare engineering error limits and evaluate dimensional temperature/displacement/tensor errors, hotspot magnitude/location, BC satisfaction, reaction/flux/energy balance, mesh-resolution sensitivity and full-trajectory drift; report worst cases as well as aggregate metrics. Separate interpolation from changed geometry/material/process and out-of-range tests. Physics losses and synthetic guard passes do not supply physical validation.
4. **Inference/twin handoff:** package checkpoint plus preprocessing/normalization, input schema, operating envelope, uncertainty/OOD/refusal policy, reference-solver fallback and measured end-to-end cost. Verify denormalization, field ID/location/frame/time mapping and chunked full-field reconstruction before OpenUSD display. Bind every predicted field to its model/case/source identities and label it as prediction. Recompute proposed design improvements with accepted reference solvers; no manufacturing or life claim follows from surrogate accuracy.

The smallest next material advance is still accepted source data and reference fields, not training. This preserves the existing material/strength → LPBF → Omniverse → PhysicsNeMo order. The current 24 poor head cells, 12 mechanical failures and missing material/physical qualification are not closed here.
