# CP1 production-material gaps for the 962 head

Research date: 18 October 2026. This English source audit adds one separate producer recipe lead and a bounded gap/request map. It selects no material or production process, changes no supplier/solver card, and executes no geometry, physics, native software, test or Git action. Local input identities are in `bindings.json`. Web observations use producer/publisher parsing, not downloaded source-byte hashes or visual PDF-table certification. No third-party document body is redistributed.

The current synthetic thermal/mechanical witnesses deliberately assume E = 71 GPa, nu = 0.33, alpha = 1e-5/K, density = 2,700 kg/m3, k = 187 W/(m K), and cp = 900 J/(kg K). Their matched-measurement fields remain null and their qualification flags false. The successful software checks do not make this combination an EOS, Nikon, Velo3D or Constellium material card.

The existing seven-recipe register and published `material-mechanical-source-refresh` remain the starting evidence. CP1's old unspecified-machine vertical 400 C/4 h row, EOS M290/M400-4, DMP Flex350Triple and the piston catalogue's Sapphire/50 micrometre route are separate states. The retained CuCrZr conductivity- and tensile-optimized treatments likewise cannot be merged; IACS is electrical conductivity. No new CP1 recipe is inserted into that register by this note.

The current [EOS CP1 page](https://www.eos.info/metal-solutions/data-sheets/all-processes-and-materials?id=eos-aluminium-constellium-cp1) still leaves modulus blank and does not provide nu or a matched hot-property card. It identifies M290 `AlCP1_060_M291` and M400-4 `AlCP1_060_M404` at TRL 3. Its interval CTE entries were already captured by the prior refresh. The live page now says status 18 October 2026 / last changed 10 September 2026. The frozen prior author/root observations reported earlier dates and 26 August; no revision or byte equivalence is asserted.

A useful NEW independent recipe lead is Nikon's [official CP1 data sheet](https://nikon-slm-solutions.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/mds5144.pdf), revision `MDS_Aheadd CP1_2024-11.2_EN`, PDF pp. 5-6. `CP1_NXG600_PRIM_MBP3_V1`, 60 micrometre, NXG XII 600/NXG 600E (1,000 W), machined vertical HT1 specimens: room-temperature yield mean/minimum 295/285 MPa, UTS 315/305 MPa, elongation 16/12%. The minimum is a 95% population/95% confidence batch tolerance interval, not a component design allowable. HT1 specifies 400 C/4 h, 20 C/min ramp in air, loading at 200 C, furnace-off cooling then removal at or below 200 C and air cooling. Optical minimum relative density is 99.8%; it is not bulk mass density. Virgin/used powder and multi-laser overlap samples are included. Keep this entire state separate; E/nu/hot laws and head qualification remain unknown.

Constellium authors' [Metal AMS 2024 abstract MAMS24-29, printed p. 75](https://metal-ams.com/app/uploads/2025/05/Web_abstractbook_MAMS24.pdf) describes CP1 Kitagawa work and dependence on defects, surface, process and postprocessing. It is a concrete route to request the underlying fatigue/defect/NDT dataset; the abstract gives no numerical S-N/TMF/creep design curves. No request was sent.

Pauzon et al., [Direct ageing of LPBF Al-1Fe-1Zr for high conductivity and mechanical performance](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.actamat.2023.119199), is a separate microstructure/ageing lead. Its [author preprint abstract](https://ssrn.com/abstract=4434705) identifies conductivity in MS/m: electrical, not a k(T) curve. The full publisher page was not reliably accessible during this pass; its complete coupon-state/curve evidence was not extracted or promoted into a card.
