## Ownership and signaling

Outer launches the wrapper once in a new session using the same selected original3.12.11 -B-I-S. Wrapper checks PID=PGID=SID and stops before caller/native execution. Outer uses exact-PID WUNTRACED stop certification and preregisters KQ_FILTER_PROC/NOTE_EXIT before raw SIGCONT, following the separately observed freshness owner055506/wrapper94a3 source pattern.

The wrapper execs the caller in that same PID. Caller launches the unchanged combined-opt argv with start_new_session=False and no preexec/session/group change. Thus caller is the one certified group leader and native inherits its group. Caller records its own PID/PGID/SID and the native PID/parent/call flags, and checks the native's PGID before any native-child reap. A missing/mismatched observation refuses; it grants no authority to signal another group. Outer joins these fields to its certificate. Remove root children()/member()/pgrep discovery and the owned-native-group set. A transient native PID is diagnostic/receipt data, never group-signaling authority.

Outer retains its strong unreaped caller-leader Popen handle through the final possible group signals. It observes exit without reaping through kqueue; no leader poll/wait/communicate/context exit/signal method/destructor may reap it prematurely. Require default SIGCHLD and no competing waiter. All raw group TERM/KILL belongs to the outer main state machine, once per phase. Its signal handlers only latch requests.

Remove the caller Timer, Lock, asynchronous stop callback, killpg and lingering-group cleanup completely. Caller SIGTERM/SIGINT handlers only latch refusal. Caller may bounded-poll/reap its own native child: that native PID is not the retained group anchor, and caller performs no subsequent signal through it. On a stop/error it writes whatever bounded partial evidence is available, without an unbounded wait. The outer still anchors and cleans the entire group if the caller exits first. No caller-native-group-absence claim is meaningful while the caller itself occupies that group.

For normal completion, outer first observes caller NOTE_EXIT and checks the complete caller/native-reaped0 receipt and the known inherited-group association. Any unexpected remaining group members require refusal/cleanup before leader reap; row discovery is evidence, not an ownership certificate. Only after deciding that no further group signals are required may outer deliberately reap its exact caller PID. Final group ESRCH is mandatory. A surviving/unknown group after reap refuses; never signal its now-unanchored numeric PGID. Before certification, cleanup may signal only the known unreaped direct PID. Registration, permission, missing-status and interruption failures remain explicit refusals.

## One shared time reference

At the earliest owner statement after importing time, capture integer monotonic_ns t0 before other imports/preflight. Pass its canonical decimal token unchanged as a narrow new owner-start-ns argument through the same-PID wrapper to the caller. This avoids a new handoff file or generic IPC controller. The fixed admission binds this runtime-argument rule; owner records its full argv/t0, caller validates a positive canonical integer that is not in the future, derives exact offsets and echoes it. Final joins require the same token, caller PID and outer certificate. Do not accept an arbitrary caller-supplied future start.

Public [CPython3.12.11 time documentation](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/python/cpython/v3.12.11/Doc/library/time.rst) describes a system-wide macOS monotonic clock and integer nanoseconds. This supports the design; it is not installed-source/ABI or a fresh clock observation. The independent root clock still covers interpreter startup through complete outer return.

Caller starts its own original100s upper-bound clock before contract preflight; its normal deadline is min(t0+95s, caller_entry+100s). It must not grant a new100s at Popen. The native wall100 remains an upper bound, never a promised allocation.

On the first stop, TERM ends at min(first_TERM+10s,t0+105s). KILL/exact reap ends no later than min(first_KILL+5s,t0+110s). These are stored once; repeated exception/finally cleanup cannot restart a grace. A late phase receives only remaining time, possibly none. After110 no new owned work or renewed wait is allowed; only bounded preservation/identity/receipt closure remains before115. No new signal follows final reap.

All ps, wait, queue and file/hash/JSON paths use the remaining shared phase time and check it again after the full operation/serialization. No unbounded wait remains. Hashes keep their original algorithms but check time between chunks and on return; directory/identity sweeps and trace parsing also check their original absolute bound. A blocking call can return late: preserve that fact and refuse rather than claiming hard real-time preemption. Forced stop can leave absent/partial caller receipts, maps or streams; mark them unknown/incomplete. Never synthesize full capture or let a prefinal candidate override late return.
