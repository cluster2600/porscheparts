# Conditional mechanical material state: evidence gap

Source-only review, 17 October 2026. **No complete mechanical material card is supported by the seven retained manufacturer recipes.** No alloy, process or head service state is selected. The existing service-input note `7fc9ecec…` and all 26 bound small files are unchanged. The three cited manufacturer PDFs were reopened through the web PDF parser; no new PDF bytes or visual table verification are claimed.

The missing condition is a *single* identifiable LPBF alloy + machine/process + orientation + heat-treatment state supplying E, Poisson ratio, expansion convention and yield at the declared test temperatures. Values from separate states cannot fill that condition:

| Retained state | What can be carried forward | What prevents a complete card |
|---|---|---|
| CP1, 400 °C / 4 h, vertical | Manufacturer yield point at 25 °C; treatment schedule | E, ν and expansion absent; the tensile row does not explicitly identify machine/layer/scan parameters |
| HT1 treatment #1 or #2, M290 / 60 µm, vertical | Distinct yield points at 25/200/250 °C | E, ν and expansion absent; both actual treatment schedules undisclosed |

The [CP1 sheet](https://assets.foleon.com/eu-central-1/de-uploads-7e3kk3/41170/product_sheet_aheadd_cp1_nov_2021docx.e81a7d073ebf.pdf), PDF p. 2 (November 2021), gives **323 MPa yield at 25 °C** for the 400 °C / 4 h vertical row. Nearby process examples are not explicitly associated with that tensile row. Its 187 W/(m·K) scalar does not supply elasticity or an expansion law; its column temperature is unspecified. Do not infer an allowable service temperature from marketing stability statements.

The [HT1 sheet](https://assets.foleon.com/eu-central-1/de-uploads-7e3kk3/41170/aheadd_ht1_fact_sheet_230620.ccac52e244fb.pdf), PDF p. 1, identifies vertical machined M290 / 60 µm coupons. Treatments #1/#2 remain separate: yield is respectively 425/285 MPa at 25 °C, 238/270 MPa at 200 °C and 188/216 MPa at 250 °C. Neither treatment's time/temperature/cooling schedule is supplied. These discrete typical yield points are not E(T), a plasticity law, fatigue life or design allowables.

The [EOS CuCrZr sheet](https://www.eos.info/var/assets/05-datasheet-images/Assets_MDS_Metal/EOS_CopperAlloy_CuCrZr/Material_DataSheet_EOS%20_Copper_CuCrZr_en.pdf?v=11), status May 2025, PDF pp. 5–7, identifies `CuCrZr_080_CoreM400`, argon and 80 µm layers. Its conductivity treatment is 550 °C / 3 h; tensile treatment 490 °C / 1 h, both with inert cooling below 100 °C. On p. 6 the horizontal/vertical E/yield pairs are 130 GPa/255 MPa and 115/230 for the former, 120/510 and 125/495 for the latter. The table cites ISO 6892-1 but supplies no numeric test temperature. Keep it null; do not call these 25 °C values or transfer M400-1 data to M290. IACS on p. 7 is electrical conductivity.
