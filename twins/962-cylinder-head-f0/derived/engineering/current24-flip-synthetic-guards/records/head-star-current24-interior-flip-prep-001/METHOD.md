## Finite association family

`prepare_context` reuses the accepted current24 intake verbatim:499 original ordered
TET4 rows,271 coordinate/hex rows,16 complete seed stars,24 poor/475 good cells and33
shared cells. It checks the original73-group partition and disjoint protection of the
prior66 applied nodes/1983 cells/86 improvements. It creates no geometry or coordinates.

`enumerate_associations` builds face and edge owner tables from ALL499 rows. It emits
one record per distinct face or edge belonging to at least one of the24 poor cells,
including missing/extra owners and nonmatching patterns. Thus there are at most
24×(4+6)=240 associations, not240 claimed valid flips. No real count is computed here.
A2→3 pattern has exactly two observed tetrahedra sharing a face and five distinct
vertices. Its opposite apices define the three edge-based replacement cells. A3→2
pattern uses the ENTIRE observed degree-three edge star: its three link edges must
form exactly the triangle on the other three vertices. It replaces that star by the
two face-based cells. No subset of a larger edge star, coordinate nudge, grid, chosen
ratio, new vertex or flip sequence is tried. Canonical node-tag order fixes record
ordering; replacement element IDs remain symbolic slots, never native mesh tags.

Owner completeness is certified from this context only when the queried simplex
contains one of the16 seeds: every tetrahedron owning it must occur in that complete
seed star. Seeing two face owners or three edge owners without such a seed does not
establish absence of outside owners. Those associations are retained as unavailable.
The introduced opposite edge/face must be absent from the complement; observed
presence fails the local compatibility check. Observed absence with no seed coverage
remains unavailable, including the corresponding global duplicate-cell question.

## Exact fixed-word local test, proposed and unrun

`screen_local_cavity` reads the original words and uses the unchanged donor Fraction
inverse-Frobenius quality kernel. Original cells must have positive determinants.
Replacement ordering is canonical except for a deterministic parity swap to obtain
positive orientation. Every replacement must satisfy D>0 and Q²≥1/100 exactly. All
replacement cells are tested, including cells without a seed; no good/shared neighbor
is dropped. There is no epsilon, relaxed gate or approximate decision.

The old and new configurations must have the same six complete oriented boundary
triangles, opposite orientations on every paired internal face, and no nonmanifold
face. For each outward boundary triangle, both other vertices must have strictly
negative exact oriented determinants. These six strict supporting-plane checks
certify the specified five-point convex triangular bipyramid; degenerate/copanar or
nonconvex configurations fail. With the2/3 pattern, positive tetrahedra and these
supporting faces, both decompositions fill that same convex cavity. Equality of the
exact signed determinant sums (six times volume) is required as a separate safeguard.
Boundary node words, boundary connectivity/orientation and every other saved cell
stay fixed. Existing saved duplicate4-sets, duplicate replacements and an introduced
simplex already present outside the cavity are refused. The pair conflict table
retains overlapping removed cells and duplicate proposed4-sets; no compatible subset
or simultaneous application is selected.

These are local domain/quality checks, conditional on the existing complex. They do
not prove the original global embedding or nonoverlap with an unseen complement.
CGAL documents the reciprocal degree-three edge/shared-face flips, convex-position
and interior restrictions in its [primary3D flip documentation](https://doc.cgal.org/latest/Triangulation_3/classCGAL_1_1Triangulation__3.html).
No CGAL installation, API call or equivalence with this held source is claimed.
