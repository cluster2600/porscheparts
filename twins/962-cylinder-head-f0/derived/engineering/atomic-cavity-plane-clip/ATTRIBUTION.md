# Source and projection attribution

Original project source, literal data and reports retain the repository terms
in `LICENSE.project`. The minimal `surface_io.hpp` is an exact extraction from
the separately hashed project `quality_patch.cpp`; its ranges are recorded.
The clipping code uses CGAL5.6 with GMP/MPFR. Third-party headers, libraries,
runtime images and executable binaries are not included or relicensed here.

Every byte-identical payload is identified in `original-records.json` by its
original repository-relative path, digest and size. Other records are explicitly
assembled allowlist projections with original record hashes; raw command and
owner/container metadata remain private. Input/artifact keys are evidence roles,
not executable host instructions. Sealed hashes verify the published bytes and
internal consistency; they do not independently authenticate a native process.

The first EPICK refusal remains ambiguous between its combined conditions.
Exact-construction fixture success does not explain that old failure or grant
head admission. The separate head proposal failed its native surface screen;
independent head geometry audits and physical qualification are not credited.
