# DDK dashboard observations for the F0 scene

Reviewed 6 November 2026. The photographed 1994 RHD 993 with driver airbag does
not establish M564 LHD geometry. No photograph is redistributed; reuse licences
are unconfirmed.

## Upper dashboard and removed components

The [21 July page](https://www.ddk-online.com/phpBB2/viewtopic.php?p=614313#p614313)
mixes author-presented and borrowed images. A later
[post on that page](https://www.ddk-online.com/phpBB2/viewtopic.php?p=614321#p614321)
identifies the centre vent as the author's own. Photos show one flanged housing,
horizontal louvers, end thumbwheels and two upper clips. The text describes end
screws, defroster-grille fasteners and a nut behind the vent: survey leads only.

The [replacement-interior post](https://www.ddk-online.com/phpBB2/viewtopic.php?p=614288#p614288)
shows separate purchased trim and a long stepped member. Its identity is
unconfirmed; its contour is not transferred into the model.

## Installed dashboard and lower-panel topology

The [22 July page](https://www.ddk-online.com/phpBB2/viewtopic.php?p=614416#p614416)
includes the later [completed-interior post](https://www.ddk-online.com/phpBB2/viewtopic.php?p=615075#p615075),
presented as the author's installation. It shows vertical side-vent blades,
horizontal centre louvers, a low cowl continuous with the pad, a shallow
passenger panel and a rounded lower band. HVAC/radio sit beneath the centre
vent; the gear-console ramp is separate. Mirroring establishes no LHD positions.

The narrative reports four lower-dashboard studs: an outer pair near side
vents and an inner pair near radio/HVAC; side vents have four clips. The 8/10 mm
values describe tools. No mounting coordinates or overall dimensions appear.
