# Front D/E hypotheses withdrawn after seating review

Date: 6 November 2026. The two front observations considered conditional D/E
candidates in the [previous review](../fastener-visibility-20261106/README.md)
are **withdrawn from the active body-crossmember contact candidates**. New
primary photographs and two fixed original-context obliques support a
cover-retained-fixing interpretation. Exact donor part, material and drivetrain
remain unconfirmed. No metric contact or donor registration is admitted.

## Evidence that changes the interpretation

An [owner's detached-cover gallery](https://www.kleinanzeigen.de/s-anzeige/porsche-911-964-993-c4-unterfahrschutz-f-vorderachse-96450412700/2168001524-223-7965)
shows retained hardware in both an arch/U-shaped seat and a circular recessed
seat. The wider H-shaped cutouts and two vent/ramp features provide coherent
panel context beyond fastener count or proximity. A separate original photograph
visibly carries the molded number `964.504.127.00`. The seller describes a C4
reference specimen; that does not establish the scanned donor's configuration
or prove an exact part match. No scale or reuse licence is supplied.

The official [Porsche PET Kat 013](https://files.porsche.com/f/332100/b26b3c7233/kat013-d-911-94-katalog.pdf)
separates three attachment families:

| Assembly reference | Observable distinction |
|---|---|
| 401-00, PDF pages 275–277 | Outer tall crossmember bosses and inner feet, with separate body-mount hex bolts. |
| 403-00, PDF pages 291–293 | Rack clamps 9 and rubber inserts 10, secured by four cylindrical-head M8 × 60 screws 11 with washers 16. These are not the body-mount hex bolts. |
| 801-45, PDF pages 421–422 | Separate underbody cover and its fixing family. A cover recess is not automatically access to a body datum. |

[Rothsport's original installed photographs](https://rothsport.com/products/steering-rack-brace-964-993)
also show the rack-clamp/brace stack. This is an assembly discriminator, not a
donor-part, year or gearbox identification. No nominal size, width, station,
symmetry or source-to-scan fit was used to choose the interpretation.

The obliques show the head/shoulder/recess-lip relationship, but neither exposes

feature. Together with the detached cover's retained hardware and panel pattern,
this warrants withdrawing the active D/E roles and retaining cover-fixing
candidates. It does **not** prove that all actual D/E mounts are absent from
the complete scan or identify a hidden surface behind the panel.
