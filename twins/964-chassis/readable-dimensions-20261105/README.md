# Readable body-dimension sources — 5 November 2026

**Result:** seven readable interior entries have been extracted from the official
964 Carrera 2 FIA form. Six are non-derived dimensions; the seventh is their
printed partial sum. A separate 993 preview gallery identifies candidate Snap-on
and Autorobot sheets. **No new jig XYZ point, fixture contact offset or tunnel
section has been recovered.** The existing [interface contract](../interface-exploitation-20261030/contract.json)
remains authoritative for engineering admission.

## Readable primary source: 964 Carrera 2, FIA B-295

The [official FIA entry](https://historicdb.fia.com/car/porsche-911-carrera-2)
links a 21-page PDF. PDF page 15, **printed page 16**, contains an interior
measurement schematic and the following values. They have been visually checked
and entered in the [documented-specification record](../../../catalog/measurements/MEAS-FIA-964-INTERIOR-20261105.json).

| Symbol | Interior reference | mm |
|---|---|---:|
| B | Height above front seats | 950 |
| C | Width at front seats | 1250 |
| D | Height above rear seats | 815 |
| E | Width at rear seats | 1190 |
| F | Steering wheel to brake pedal | 563 |
| G | Steering wheel to rear bulkhead | 1420 |
| H | Printed F + G | 1983 |

These are homologation definitions, not bare-body attachment coordinates.
Seat-related heights are not floor-to-roof Z dimensions. F and G have different
directions in the schematic: their sum is not a straight pedal-to-bulkhead span.
The interior page supplies no tolerances; article 210 on PDF page 2 separately
prints ±1% for G. This is not measurement uncertainty or a jig tolerance.
They can check later interior packaging once the measurement definitions and
vehicle configuration match; they cannot locate seat rails, pedals, steering
mounts or a tunnel surface. The donor year/gearbox remain unknown.

Two separately identified forms provide a limited documentary comparison:
[964 Carrera RS B-296](https://historicdb.fia.com/car/porsche-911-carrera-rs),
PDF page 15, repeats B–H;
[993 Carrera RS 3.8 GT2-004](https://historicdb.fia.com/car/porsche-911-carrera-rs-38-993),
PDF page 9, repeats B and C only. Agreement does not establish body or mounting
interchangeability. The source PDFs are retained privately; hashes, lengths and
reviewed pages are in [the inventory](inventory.json). FIA provides these forms
for consultation, not as certified event documents or approval of a new design.

## More specific jig-sheet leads

The [CarGeometry.eu 993 listing](https://cargeometry.eu/PORSCHE-911-993-Carrera-1993-1998-p173268837)
has six technical previews, all downloaded through publicly linked image URLs
and visually reviewed. This is a different gallery from the previously audited
964 `.org`/`.com` images; common ownership is not assumed.

| Preview group | Observed content | Native raster and limitation |
|---|---|---|
| 01–03 | Snap-on copyright; Carrera 2DR AWD heading; openings, longitudinal/height stations and floor diagonals | 560 × 396 px each; no new numeric value admitted |
| 04–05 | Autorobot; Porsche 911 Carrera 2/4, Type 993; upper and lower measurement/contact diagrams | 276 × 380 px each; small codes and values unresolved |
| 06 | Autorobot fixture setup | 270 × 380 px; equipment offsets would not themselves be vehicle coordinates |

The seller advertises three multipage PDFs, including a separate Turbo file,
for EUR 9 and says the previews are reduced. This is **an unverified commercial
lead**, not an acquired original or permission for engineering reuse. None of
the six reviewed previews establishes Turbo-specific coverage. Small page and
reference numbers are deliberately not guessed. Enlarging pixels adds no detail.

The [manufacturer's Autorobot service](https://www.autorobot.com/support-and-service/autorobot-measuring-data-service/)
provides nominal sheets, vehicle-specific setup information and measurement-point
photographs via subscription. The visible 993-labelled previews now identify a
specific candidate to check with that publisher; its current 964/993 availability,
revision and reuse terms remain unconfirmed. The target is an authorised PDF/ADF
with its datum, contact symbols, assembly state and fixture-offset definitions.

## What advances and what still blocks geometry

- **Interior reference:** seven traceable scalar entries are available for later
  packaging checks, with no invented XYZ or metrological precision.
- **Nominal body points:** pursue the original 993-labelled sheets, and the
  corresponding 964 C2 sheets, including contact photographs and datum rules.
- **Tunnel, door-foot and wheelhouse surfaces:** still require a lawful scan or
  dimensioned section dataset. None of these scalar sheets establishes surfaces.
- **Simulation:** no new geometry, material assignment or structural solve is
  justified by this source review alone. Engineering and manufacturing gates
  remain open.

No purchase or additional supplier message was made during this review. The
previously authorised TetraVision enquiry remains a separate acquisition route.
