## Fixed scope and actual result

faces: 3355000, 3456510 and 3370117. These remain fastening-like, upper-round and
cylinder-like observations, not identified body contacts. All acquired Z layers
were retained within the fixed region in the provisional scan frame. No Mi or

The offline CPU run used one CPU and a 4 GiB cap, completed in 9.65 seconds
without timeout or OOM, and removed its container. These are CPU VTK diagnostics,

## Anatomical finding and limits

The author and independent reviewer actually inspected both cut views and the
six-panel section image. Additional curved/cylinder-like and sheet surfaces
become readable behind the earlier opaque context. Neither review positively
traces the cylinder to a fixed-body flange, its bearing seat and the companion
M8 head/washer pair. The separate vertical carrier fastening, trim and adjacent
mechanical surfaces remain alternatives. No Mi J, CH contact or exact PET item
is admitted from these layers.

Contact identity, physical head boundary, centres, bolt axes, calibration,
probe uncertainty and a proper common-frame transform remain unqualified.
This finite result does not establish global scan absence or that all acquired
heads are unmeasurable. No head fit, repaired surface, registered body, new
coque, manufacturing or road/track qualification follows from it. Raw arrays,
coordinates, images and section archives remain private.
