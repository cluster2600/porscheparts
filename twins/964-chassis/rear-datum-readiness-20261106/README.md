# Rear datum source and visibility review

This follow-up reviews alternative documentary contacts and eight new rear
context images. It adds **no qualified scan contact or registered body**. The
earlier [correspondence ledger](../dimensional-correction-20261106/correspondence/README.md)
remains unchanged. [assessment.json](assessment.json) binds the source references,
original feature IDs, private execution receipts and image hashes.

## Source definitions

The [owner records](../owner-frame-data-20261105/source-data.json) retain separate
CH, Mi, Ed and Ar frames and contact states. CH's explicit vehicle heading is a
1992 Carrera 2 Coupe. Its points 12/13 remain **bolt pictograms in both parts-on
and parts-removed sheets**, with unchanged printed heights. They must not be
relabelled as bare-body holes because the second sheet says parts removed.

CH 12 at the outer rear region and CH 13 inboard support graphical hypotheses
involving PET 501-00 plate/strut 7 with bolt 6, and arm 1 with bolt 5. Neither
sheet explicitly equates its numbered point to a PET item. Similar coordinates
or span values do not establish that link.

The official [Snap-on/Blackhawk Quik Chek guide](https://www.snaponequipment.com/Common/UserManuals/ZEECR610A.pdf)
defines exposed head/stud-tip centres and distinguishes hole/slot side probing;
its printed page 23 illustrates flat/concave heads and different rim forms.
The retained Ed leaves explicitly carry a Snap-on copyright, and show distinct
25 × 30 slot and 24 hole contacts at points 7/8. Their exact vehicle/year header,
export-system linkage and donor applicability remain unconfirmed. The guide
does not authenticate a CH, Ar or Other convention or supply Porsche tolerances.

CH's boxed 322 width and dimension placement are consistent with indexed text
from a [Chief Vector manual](https://www.vsg-asia.com/uploads/5779f8bf/VectorUsers4-03.pdf)
describing a boxed Body Zero width. This is a conditional format association:
direct PDF retrieval failed, its figures were not inspected, and the retained
CH PDF metadata identifies a screen-capture exporter rather than a publisher.
Ar's setup/tool offsets and Other's symbol/bracket conventions remain unresolved.
Other's I/J letters also differ from Mi's rear-contact roles; J is a wrench
glyph without a printed 17. No source frames are merged.

## What the new images establish

Both four-view sets retain the same 2,807,208 original rear triangles with full
acquired Y/Z, opaque and unchanged. The inspection domain contains whole
triangles intersecting X ≤ −1500 in the provisional scan frame. These eight
rendered images are not eight acquisitions and do not expose unscanned surfaces.

The pivot views acquire cast arms, hubs, bush/bracket regions and anti-roll
hardware. A short cylindrical feature initially considered near the outer
pivot is connected to a thin **round** member, unlike the broad flat PET 501-00
plate 7. Its original witnesses are retained as an anti-roll/link-family
alternative, and that particular CH 12 interpretation is withdrawn. This does
not reject every possible CH 12/13 feature elsewhere in the scan. No complete
named body bracket, bushing and source bolt head is qualified by these views.

The final four views specifically inspect lower rear bumper supports from
inboard and outboard on both sides. PET 802-05 distinguishes the body-side
M8 bolt pair 9 with washers 8 from the vertical M12 carrier bolt 2 with washer
3, and from trim attachments. A small upright bracket/fastening feature is
acquired in the positive-Y outboard view, but the absorber-tube-to-body-flange
connection and companion-head seating are not established. It is not admitted
as Mi J, a CH point, or an exact PET part. The other views retain incomplete
sheet, exhaust and trim context; they do not authenticate the required pair.

## Remaining admission boundary

Source-contact identity, head/rim construction, scan calibration, source/probe
uncertainty and redundant off-plane checks must precede a rigid registration.
Current centres, bolt axes, source-to-scan transformation and acceptance limits
remain unknown. The positive acquisition and rejected alternatives are finite
inspection results, not proof that the entire scan lacks usable contacts or
that those contacts are globally unmeasurable.

Raw scans, manual pages and scan images remain private. No material card,
new body geometry, 993 compatibility, mold, structural qualification or native
Omniverse validation is added here.
