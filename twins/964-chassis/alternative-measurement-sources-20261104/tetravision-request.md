# TetraVision data availability enquiry

Status: **unsent draft**, prepared 4 November 2026. This is an availability and
licensing enquiry only, with no order, budget commitment or attached private data.

To: info@tetravision.be

Subject: Porsche 964 chassis scan — data availability and coverage

Hello TetraVision team,

I found your video “3D scan of a Porsche 964 chassis”, published on 22 November
2024: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9x_SR-ZGiCs.

We are developing a research digital twin for a carbon structural body cell
compatible with the Porsche 964, with 993 compatibility assessed separately.
We already have an assembled underbody scan and Porsche body-control spans,
but need interior surfaces and positively identified structural mounting
interfaces. Our original reference vehicle is no longer available for a survey.

Is the existing dataset available for licensed engineering use with your
client's permission? If not, could you arrange access to another suitable
reference body for measurement? Before discussing an order, could you confirm:

- The body style, model year, Carrera 2/4 configuration and modifications of the
  scanned body, without sharing its VIN or customer details.
- Whether the files cover the interior tunnel, footwells, door/sill structures,
  front/rear bulkheads, wheelhouses and structural mounting faces/holes, including
  any unobserved or reconstructed regions. A coverage preview would be useful.
- The original point cloud/mesh formats, units, reference frame, scanning and
  registration method, independent dimensional checks and stated uncertainty.
  If CAD is available, please distinguish measured surfaces from inferred ones.
- The permitted uses: internal reverse engineering, derived CAD and simulation,
  commercial development, and publication of limited derived views. Please
  identify any client permission needed. We are not requesting AI-training rights.

Please indicate whether 993 reference data is also available as a separate
dataset. We can provide a precise interface checklist if useful. This enquiry
does not authorise chargeable work or a purchase.

Thank you.
