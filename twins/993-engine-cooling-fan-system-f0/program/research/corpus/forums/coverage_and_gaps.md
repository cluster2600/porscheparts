## Community coverage

- Pelican Parts: strongest original measurement and modification material. Full first-page text read for several core threads. Some subsequent pages returned cache misses, Unicode decoding errors or a 403. No attempt to bypass that 403. Search-index extracts are distinguished in source records.
- Rennlist: substantive original-post extracts identified model/part distinctions, corrosion, clearance, bearings and balance. Some direct opens returned internal errors. No claim of full archive coverage.
- PFF: German originals and site-supplied English automatic translations supplied part-family and crack discussions. The translation origin is explicit for F14; German original text was also recovered via a quoted linked post. Several full opens failed despite rich indexed excerpts.
- Type911: French owner blogs and technical article found. Main fan rotor was distinguished from rear alternator cap/deflector and auxiliary blowers. No full Type911 archive inspection.
- PorscheMania: public index was visible in cloud browser; three selected topics fully read. Index explicitly advertises separate members-only technical areas; these were not accessed. Two further observed topic URLs remain useful leads: https://www.porschemania.it/discus/messages/488172/812872.html?1496833073 (993 dubbio ventolone/raffreddamento) and https://www.porschemania.it/discus/messages/488172/715140.html?1416289568 (Ventola Porsche 993). Their contents were not read and are not evidence here.
- CARTUNE: useful Japanese owner report indexed, full open cache miss. No brand inferred.
- Minkara: relevant replacement note identified, limited indexed access. No numeric-clearance claim promoted.
- DDK: public power discussion retrieved as substantial indexed text. An initially found post-specific URL failed direct open; canonical thread result later provided context.
- TheSamba / Shoptalkforums / Flat4ever / GermanLook: VW conversion discussions located. Do not transfer their results to a factory six-cylinder shroud. Two useful but limited TheSamba leads are retained; French Flat4ever horizontal conversion https://www.flat4ever.com/forums/topic/56258-turbine-porsche-a-plat-sur-bloc-t1/ opened with cache miss, so not promoted to validated evidence.
- ImpactBumpers: an early Carrera 3.0 ownership thread surfaced qualitative five-blade sound commentary, but no stronger original measurement was recovered. Relevant lead: https://www.impactbumpers.com/forum/index.php?%2Ftopic%2F24211-introducing-pauls-911-carrera-30-coupe%2F=&comment=271767&do=findComment
- Early911S Registry: 245 mm wanted advertisement, finish discussions and racing-engine references located; no original calibrated fan test recovered. Example lead: https://www.early911sregistry.org/forums/showthread.php?77602-WTB-245mm-11-blade-fan=&s=2fdc05af6f9b1e1897951719effddb70
- PistonHeads: general 993 buying-guide and modified-car threads found; no fan-specific measurement record promoted.
- 911uk / Porsche Club GB: coating, clearance and ageing-fan reports discovered; not deeply inspected enough for the core claim ledger. Leads: https://911uk.com/porsche/another-distributor-and-fan-renovation.103519/ and https://www.porscheclubgb.com/forum/threads/powder-coating-or-keracoting-the-cooling-fan.126119/post-126119
- Club911: French index and unrelated technical documents surfaced; no substantive main-rotor forum thread recovered in this pass. This is a search gap, not evidence the community lacks information.
- Elferteam: native German queries returned indirect references, but no suitable accessible original thread. No exhaustive or current-access claim.

## What was not established

1. A production-ready 911/935 blade solid, drawing, airfoil section or GD&T specification
2. A verified 250 mm rotor family, or a resolved meaning for every 257/279.4 mm number encountered
3. A complete 964/993/993 Turbo alternator-specific interchange matrix or current part-number supersession list
4. A calibrated, uncertainty-bounded Q–Δp–shaft-power map for each factory variant
5. Factory radial tip-clearance tolerances, balance grade, stress allowables, modal data or safe overspeed limit
6. A completed controlled OEM-versus-billet/composite rotor efficiency or durability comparison
7. A completed 935 flat-versus-upright six-cylinder temperature-mapping report from the prototype projects located
8. A statistically meaningful corrosion or cracking failure rate
