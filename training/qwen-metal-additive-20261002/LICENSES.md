# Attribution and third-party licenses

Original preparation scripts and original project material follow the
repository license. **The repository's proprietary license does not replace
or restrict the CC BY 4.0 rights in the third-party articles and their excerpts.**

The seven article snapshots, cleaned documents and CPT excerpts retain their
original authors and [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)
license. The per-source records in `input/documents.json` identify authors,
title, DOI/URL, license evidence, access date, transformation and hashes.
License text/evidence is in `input/licenses/`; originals are in
`input/snapshots/`. Required attribution accompanies each CPT row through its
matching provenance record and source ID.

Transformations: HTML tags and navigation removed, scientific text extracted,
references/administrative sections excluded, whitespace normalized and text
segmented with overlap. Mathematical notation and tables may be flattened.
No document translation is presented as an original publication. Synthetic
multilingual SFT answers are generated educational examples, not quotations.

Some MDPI HTML `dc.rights` metadata states CC BY 3.0 while the article's explicit
Copyright section links CC BY 4.0; that section and the complete snapshot are
retained as the licensing evidence, rather than silently changing the source.
Third-party material explicitly excluded by an article's credit line retains
its own rights; inspect such notices before reusing those elements.

All other research entries are bibliographic metadata and original research
summaries only. No unknown-license raw full text, proprietary manual, raw scan,
credentials, vehicle identifier or model weights are added. HAL deposit access
and NIST federal authorship are not treated as automatic copyright clearance.
