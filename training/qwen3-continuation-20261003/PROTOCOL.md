# Qwen3 continuation: registered protocol

Status: numerical recovery before scientific evaluation. Candidates A and B
were rejected for non-finite metrics/gradients. The reserved set remains closed;
no scientific improvement is demonstrated. Original registrations are retained,
with explicitly authorized numerical amendments in [protocol.json](protocol.json).

## Problem and scope

The historical Qwen3 adapter scored 11/12 primary versus 12/12 for its paired
base; its 7/8 supplemental score has no base control. All earlier questions,
answers, source paragraphs and profile experiments are development information.
Recorded errors include omission of a requested gradual transition and an
unsupported dominant-influence claim. Independent conservative review also
flags a condition from one study attached to percentages from another; its
6/8 supplemental judgement does not overwrite the historical recorded 7/8. The compact curriculum also paraphrases
"often due to" without its frequency qualifier. See the unchanged
[historical report](../qwen-metal-additive-20261002/FIDELITY_QWEN3.md).

This experiment concerns evidence-conditioned scientific question answering for
the project. It does not train a numerical solver, qualify material properties,
establish a causal model or validate manufacture. Qwen2.5-Coder/MLX is a separate
pilot and supplies no weights, scores or success claim to this experiment.

The new curriculum uses original, already licensed CC BY 4.0 passages from the
six historical training source families. Preserve attribution, source and

The impeller corpus is an evidence ledger, not a collection of established
physical facts. Record each source's reuse eligibility, claim level,
contradictions and unknown measurements. No impeller prose enters this SFT
candidate. Any future use requires item-specific rights and content review.

Keep the exact Qwen/Qwen3-4B-Instruct-2507 revision, original rank-8 q/v adapter
and task_routed_v14 inference helper. Train and evaluate with that same expanded
profile. Do not tune the profile or decoding during this experiment. Base,
historical adapter and new candidate use the same source excerpts, expanded
messages, tokenizer and greedy 192-token limit in one pinned runtime. The base
control disables PEFT adapters. A backend change must be recorded and all paired
controls regenerated there; historical predictions cannot act as its control.

## Bounded training and development

Candidate A continues the actual historical adapter with one epoch on at most
24 rows, learning rate 1e-5, accumulation 2, seed 42 and at most 12 optimizer
steps. Save checkpoints every four steps and the final adapter. Maximum two
threads, 18 GiB process RSS, 30 minutes training; stop for non-finite loss,
resource excess or deadline. Stop only the experiment's own process. Use no
new rental, credentials, permissions or service changes. The original two-candidate ceiling governs A/B. Both registrations and rejected
receipts are retained. The parent explicitly authorized a separate numerical
recovery C before any reserved exposure; this does not retrospectively expand
A/B or authorize selecting a new candidate after reserved results.

Pre-training amendment: the original CPU registration is preserved in
[protocol-cpu-registration.json](protocol-cpu-registration.json). The official
PyTorch MPS probe completed 16-token inference and one forward/backward pass
without optimizer updates. The entire CPU probe exceeded its 600-second bound;
it supplies no completed CPU timing or CPU/MPS token-parity result. Candidate A
therefore uses MPS BF16, with a conservative live RSS + Metal-driver allocation
ceiling of 18 GiB and at least 16 GiB available memory before loading. Every
scientific control is regenerated on MPS. This is a new backend relative to the
historical Linux experiment, not reproduction of its numerical execution.

Numerical amendment C uses eager attention without automatic autocast, BF16
transformer weights and FP32 LoRA parameters. The official source config declares
tied embeddings. Preserve its on-disk bytes and declared tie flag; construct a
distinct frozen FP32 output projection as an exact numerical copy on CPU before
MPS transfer. Cast only inputs to that projection to FP32. Verify BF16 input
embedding raw hashes unchanged, no alias after separation, frozen input/output
parameters, actual devices/dtypes and projection hash after PEFT, before/after
training and in each evaluation arm. This records a computation/precision change,
not a claim that the historical architecture/runtime is unchanged. Any resulting
comparison concerns adapter plus policy C; every control uses policy C too.

The preliminary direct `.lm_head.to(FP32)` diagnostic did not prove that tied
input embeddings remained BF16. Treat it as a precision diagnostic only. Two
separate-copy zero-update probes stopped at the original conservative 18 GiB
ceiling; their failures are immutable. A newly registered limited 24 GiB bound
was authorized after measuring 33.13 GiB available RAM and zero current swap on
the 64 GiB Mac. Monitor live worker RSS plus MPS driver allocation, active tensor
allocation, system availability and swap. Stop the experiment if available RAM
falls below 16 GiB or swap grows more than 256 MiB. No other process is stopped.
The complete twenty-row pre-training gate uses the original historical adapter,
zero optimizer updates and a 300-second deadline. All losses and trainable
gradients must be finite. This is a numerical gate, not scientific evaluation.

## Independent reserved evaluation

A custodian independent of data authoring holds at least 24 newly authored
questions and rubrics, including at least eight critical items. Cover requested
qualifiers, property-condition pairing, units, causal restraint, source support,
conflicting/unknown evidence and part-specific qualification. Include project
impeller evidence-level questions only from authored ledger facts or reusable
sources. Familiar articles/concepts and unknown base pretraining exposure remain
explicit limitations. A fresh paragraph alone is not a new scientific domain.

Before generation the custodian publishes a hash commitment and coverage counts,
without revealing prompts or gold answers to the training lead. The locked
candidate and base receive identical inputs. Run every item in both arms, with
the same decoding and runtime, preserving actual responses, token counts,
budget hits, input hashes, weights, timings and exceptions. Partial runs fail
closed. The custodian supplies the prompts and rubrics after model selection;
the lead does not adapt data, weights or prompts in response. A retest needs
new independent items, and these items then become development.

Two independent assistant reviewers judge each anonymized response against its
source and rubric. Any disagreement is conservatively a failure for acceptance;
retain both judgements and reasons. A response passes only if all requested
source-backed facts and qualifiers are present, property/unit/condition pairs
are correct, citations support the asserted claim, and no unsupported causal,
dominance, certification or safety claim appears. Correct refusals must name the
missing evidence relevant to the task; generic refusals fail factual questions.
This remains assistant review, not independent human engineering validation.

Acceptance requires at least 22 scientifically acceptable candidate responses
out of the minimum 24, a paired net gain of at least two acceptable responses,
all critical items acceptable, zero critical regressions, all citations supported
and zero generation-budget hits. With larger reserves, retain at least 11/12
acceptable responses as a fraction. Report gains, losses, ties and an exact
two-sided McNemar diagnostic; the small sample does not establish broad or
statistically significant benefit. A failed threshold means no adoption, even
if loss decreases. Record the result without inventing universal success.
