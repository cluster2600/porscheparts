# Public references for the supports — 16 October 2026

The [manufacturer JP Group](https://jpgroup.dk/en/p/engine-mount-left-right/15376768/)
links its DANSK CLASSIC part number `1617900700` to `99337504909` and to the
993 Turbo 3.6 from March 1995 to September 1997. Its page provides no stiffness
matrix, load–displacement curve or frequency and temperature law.

The [Rennline M154](https://www.rennline.com/rs-engine-mount/) page publishes
75 Shore A for the non-Turbo 964/993 variants and explicitly excludes the
Turbo. This hardness was therefore not used to assign a stiffness to the
studied carrier. A material hardness does not, by itself, define the
directional stiffness of an assembly.

The calculations keep two explicit conventions: rigid supports for
the reference comparison, and `K1000` for the study with compliant supports. In
the latter case, **1,000 N/mm is the total per eye and per axis**, distributed
among its nodes. It is not a value attributed to the Porsche mount by these
public pages. The results of these two conventions remain identified in
the tables and figures.

The references, observations and unpublished fields are recorded in
[the research receipt](boundary-condition-research.json).
