# Normalisation of holes covered by the intentional construction

`exact_intended_holes.normalize_holes(result, primitives, allowed)` returns a
candidate and a receipt. It keeps the coordinates of the exterior and of the
retained holes; it creates no vertex and calls no polygon overlay.

A convex hole may be removed only if two proofs pass: its
whole set belongs to the allowed domain, and every cell of its rational
partition belongs to at least one primitive of the intentional union.
The partition uses every line of the primitives' boundaries; its
areas are preserved exactly. Membership is constant inside
each cell. Since the finite union of the primitives is closed, it also contains
every boundary of the covered cells.

Holes that are non-convex, partially covered, genuinely absent from the
primitives or outside the allowed domain remain unchanged. No minimum size
or area tolerance is involved. The digests of the primitives must be
linked to those of the domain and of the real sweeps by the builder.

The receipt `status=completed` describes this local operation, without admitting the
support. After normalisation, the proofs `D ⊆ candidate ⊆ allowed` remain
mandatory, followed by triangulation, remapping, contacts, connectivity and
a full audit of the toolpaths actually exposed.

The [skin576 witness](intended-hole-evidence/skin576-assessment.json) keeps
the rejection of the initial IEEE result despite an empty GEOS difference. Its four
triangular holes are each covered entirely by `D ∪ sweeps` and
`allowed`. Their certified removal passes both inclusions, with every
vertex of the exterior unchanged. This witness remains a local planar
construction; no complete support was regenerated by this check.
