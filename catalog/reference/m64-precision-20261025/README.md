# M64 guide, clearance and interface research, 2026-10-25

Twenty grouped documentary records extend the prior research: 17 qualified
declarations and three records that teach uncertainty. Ten source
representations are linked; seven canonical sources are new and three are
reused without editing their frozen records. No physical component was measured.

See the [research report](../../../docs/research/m64-precision-20261025/README.md)
and [prepared training data](../../../training/m64-precision-20261025/README.md).

The factory rod matching rule has a restricted Type3 M64/60 applicability.
Carrera/RS shaft dimensions remain comparison references. Metelli, Würth,
Carrillo, LN and ARP declarations retain their named aftermarket applications.
Nominal sizes, machining references, loaded bolt extension and unloaded growth
are separate measurement kinds. None qualifies a physical assembly or grants
machining approval. Raw documents, workbook files, HTML and reader responses,
page images and copied source text are not redistributed.
