# 935 head reconstruction — private research archive

This archive preserves the 26–27 September 2026 CAD-Recode/Omniverse session and the 24 OpenClaw research missions. The target is the original Wolfe 935 head scan. FVD 993 photos were used only for comparison.

- [Controlled research synthesis](research/SYNTHESE.md) and 24 unapproved source reports.
- [Reconstruction report](REPRISE.md), [original session report](RESULTATS.md), and [file hashes](manifest.json).
- [Analytic circular profiles](run/fresh-v2/recovered/circular-profiles.step) and [partial planar faces](run/fresh-v2/surfaces-repaired/planar-faces.step).
- [Repair overview](run/fresh-v2/repair-inspection.png), [repair detail](run/fresh-v2/repair-detail.png), and [partial CAD surfaces](run/fresh-v2/surface-inspection-repaired.png).

285 small scan gaps were patched. The mesh still has 95,686 boundary edges; this is not a closed head solid. The recovered geometry contains 22 circular profiles and 14 planar faces. Physical units, exact variant and material remain unverified. Nothing here authorizes manufacture or engine operation.

The original OBJ, repaired mesh, equivalent raw-scan USD layers, model weights, credentials and VM state remain outside Git. Derived CAD is retained in this private repository for the owner's research; redistribution rights have not been established. Historical report links to omitted mesh/USD files refer to the full local backup under `work/cad-recode-935-fresh/vm-results/`.

The scripts in `research/` preserve the one-off VM research run; they are historical operator scripts, not an automatic deployment service. Raw OpenClaw JSON responses and state are omitted; public findings and execution receipts are retained. The VM's absence was verified through the approved Vast wrapper after expiry.
