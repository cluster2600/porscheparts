# 935 horizontal fan: bottom-up line frozen, top-down twin started

Decided by the owner on 2026-11-06, recorded 2026-11-09.

## Decisions

1. **Goal:** two flat fans. The original 935 fan, rebuilt faithfully from
   the scan, and a new horizontal fan for the 993 in the spirit of the
   Gunther Werks flat-fan cars. The 993 fan is an original design.
2. **Scale:** one scan unit is one millimetre, declared by the owner. It is
   recorded as an owner declaration (`H-SCALE` in the twin's hypothesis
   register), not as an instrument calibration, and no longer blocks work.
3. **Freeze:** the triangle-level repair, mesh-trial and fragment-guard line
   documented in this folder (about 150 commits, 3 October to 6 November
   2026) is frozen. Its records stay unchanged; no new study of that kind
   starts. The CFD verdict in
   [`admissibility.json`](../../../twins/935-horizontal-cooling-system-f0/admissibility.json)
   keeps governing the frozen surface.

## Why

After a month, the rotor's tip diameter, hub bore and mounting pattern were
still unrecorded, because every value waited on a scale calibration, and the
rotor surface was repaired facet by facet until the CFD admissibility audit
showed the failing facets lay beyond the acquired sections. The same pattern
led the owner to freeze the 935 head repair line on the same day.

The rotor is a revolved hub plus one blade repeated nine times. That shape
fits well top-down: in millimetres, datum A is flat to 0.10 mm RMS and the
bore fits to 0.16 mm RMS on the first pass.
