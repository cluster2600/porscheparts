## Conclusion

The reconstruction sources, the studies under assumptions and the protocols
are already integrated into `main`. What remains is to produce **the
calibrated whole rotor, the assembled mechanism and the installed airflow
system**, then their calculations and tests. The current result is a partial
reconstruction with verified tools; it is not a manufacturable assembly or a
validated twin.

The first target remains a replica of the **935** installation. The
horizontal adaptation to a **993** engine comes next; the vertical 993 fan
remains separate.

## What the software allows today

The [coordinator](../../../twins/935-horizontal-cooling-system-f0/source/run_reconstruction.py)
runs `preflight`, `surfaces`, `picogk`, `cad`, `review`, `hub`, `cad-hub`,
`drive`, `cad-drive`, `support` and `cad-support`. It does not yet implement
the CFD, system FEA, optimisation or training chain. The
[solver dossier](../../../twins/935-horizontal-cooling-system-f0/solver-handoff.json)
describes their required inputs; it does not launch the calculations.

Full integration therefore does not come down to installing more software.
The geometry and interfaces must be defined, then the solver calls and their
checks added to the coordinator. Reuse the existing programs and runtimes
before any new installation. The historical preflight did not find OpenMDAO
or PhysicsNeMo in the environments examined; their current availability
remains to be re-checked.

## Proposed order of work

| Priority | Action and concrete deliverable | Closure criterion |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | Requalify Linux access and the scientific imports, then the PicoGK/FreeCAD witnesses of the chosen runtime. | Environment identified, imports and witnesses run, versions and receipts kept before restarting the reconstruction. |
| 1 | Finish the rotor/bracket coverage map and the dimension register. Fit the missing observed areas; compare the scale scenarios without voxelising each assumption. | Measured/interpolated/redrawn areas identified, traceable transforms and two independent dimensions per scan to accept a scale. |
| 2 | Complete the editable rotor, hub and bracket; define the priority rotor/hub/shaft and bracket/engine interfaces. Build a specific dossier for the angle drive internals. | Solids needed for the calculation, overlays/sections and deviations; analytic interfaces, retentions and clearances documented. A redrawn internal area keeps that status and does not prove an exact replica. |
| 3 | Produce the real mechanical bill of materials, the assembly poses and the transmission diagram. Identify pulleys, gear teeth, bearings, coupling, oil and fasteners. | Signed ratios and directions, supports and load paths, interference check and access for assembly/maintenance. |
| 4 | Add an OpenFOAM MRF pilot then its curves, with defined housing and clearances. Add the loads to CalculiX and the shaft/support mechanics. | Conservation, quality and convergence on three meshes; independent analytical checks; results tied to the exact geometry and case. |
| 5 | Prepare part-by-part manufacturing and the system's standalone bench. | Dimensioned/toleranced drawings, print or machining routing, treatments, inspections, balancing and mechanical review; bench limits and instrumentation defined. |
| 6 | Assemble the USD twin and compare predictions with reserved bench points before calibration. | Consistent geometry, units, joints and results; correlation with uncertainties and documented limits of use. Physical operation waits for these measurements. |

**Work possible without waiting for the forums:** reconstruction of the
remaining observed surfaces, comparison of conditional scales, propagation of
these scenarios into masses/inertias/loads and preparation of the solver
cases. The assumptions must remain named, revisable and separate from the
historical reference. A nominal angle drive study can progress this way; it
does not complete the historical definition of the hidden internals.

OpenMDAO will coordinate calculations that are already accepted. PhysicsNeMo
will come after a set of calculations has been built with geometries reserved
for testing. Neither replaces the reconstruction or the validation of the
solvers. Fan optimisation and the 993 adaptation follow the 935 reference.
The [993 F2 horizontal study](../../993/993_FLAT_FAN_SIZING_F2.md) already
exists in `main`, but remains a sizing on synthetic requirements: it does not
calibrate the 935 scans or their interfaces.

## Reading the evidence and checks

The final variations in volume (0.1204 %) and inertia (0.1412 %) concern only
the reconstructed blade ranges, not the complete rotor. The 1.54 % stress
deviation in the CalculiX tests qualifies the ideal ring witness, not the
blade roots. The 66.19 N·m calculated at 8,500 rpm with a stop in 0.1 second
depends on the assumed reduced AlSi10Mg rotor. This result justifies
preparing the transient loads, without defining those of the real mechanism.

The [`main` CI at commit `b9062126`](https://github.com/cluster2600/porscheparts/actions/runs/38234896127)
ran `make check` successfully: 3,658 main tests, then additional checks.
This software check does not rerun the physical calculations. The historical
checks and their versions remain in the linked reports. Kali2 was not
reachable at its previously used address during the check of 12 October; the
Linux CI provides the repository verification. Targeting 60 935 tests on the
Mac hit eight import errors of the native SciPy extension of the user Python,
with ten tests skipped. This scientific environment must be requalified;
this run does not constitute a geometric validation and no dependency was
modified.

The community follow-up of 10 October found no written reply after our
Pelican/Rennlist messages and one "like" on Rennlist. The replies and the DDK
activation were not re-checked for this status. The forums serve to obtain
identifiable information; they must not suspend the geometric work or replace
metrology. The [bench protocol](BENCH_PROTOCOL.md) remains to be run after the
mechanical review.
