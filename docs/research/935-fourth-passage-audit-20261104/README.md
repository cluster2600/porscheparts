## Question and fixed inputs

stations. Its fourth search region contained observations, but failed the
exploratory circle criteria. Is that failure an absence of observations, an
open scan contour, or a mismatch with the circle model?

Replay the **existing, unfiltered fourth-region source-face selections** at all
three historical stations. Their hashes, the original decoded-array hash and
the original search-frame hash are fixed in the driver before execution. The
search region and station names are inherited from the earlier private study;
they are not new anatomical datums or physical measurements. No symmetry,
catalogue dimension, normal filtering, trimming or new search window is used.
The driver retains every selected face segment, including the short fragment.

within-scan checks, **not physical metrology**. The result near 0.4 mm is not a
physical tolerance decision. Different point selections, splitting and fitting

The closed first profile supplies real observations, but closure alone does not
prove circularity. The open deeper profiles do not authorize a complete cylinder,

## Independent verification and controls

The verifier reads the actual three NPZ outputs and the original acquired arrays.
It checks every selected original face exactly once, inverts the frame, projects
each actual endpoint onto its recorded original **XYZ edge**, checks the section
plane, edge membership and interpolation interval, and independently constructs
the connectivity graph with SciPy. It checks all endpoint incidences against
the complete original mesh. All six verification checks pass; maximum edge and
plane discrepancies are recorded in the [aggregate receipt](audit.json).

Five producer controls pass: a closed loop, an open chain, distinct source-edge
identities, a known triangle-plane cut and refusal of altered legacy support.
Three additional reason-specific controls refuse an altered output coordinate,
wrong original-array hash and an existing output directory. Invalid inputs
publish no successful result. The independent verifier does not certify circle
fit quality or physical dimensions.

Both successful stages ran under the existing
[local-only sandbox profile](../935-observed-wall-cad-20261104/source/local-only.sb)
with a 180-second process bound. Actual execution took under one second per
stage on the Mac. Refusal controls ran separately without a network claim.
The unchanged original OBJ hash was rechecked. No model inference, external
service, supplier contact or paid compute was used for this audit.
