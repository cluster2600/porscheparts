diameter and 3 mm projection. These remain author declarations, not measurements

material behaviour and professional review remain necessary.

slicer feedback plus an end-plate revision intended to avoid earlier 97%
scaling. These are print reports, not a calibrated unit or universal correction
factor. Do not merge original/Mk4 interfaces or promote reported print strength
to a structural or clutch-system acceptance.
