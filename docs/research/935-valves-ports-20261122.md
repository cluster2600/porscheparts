# 935 head twin: valves, seats, guides, hidden geometry and port flow, a search of 22 November 2026

Issues #697, #698 and #700. On 2026-11-22 the owner asked for the gaps of these
three issues to be filled from published data under declared assumptions;
review will be by the seller of the scanned head, and nobody was contacted.
**Nothing here is a measurement of the scanned head.** Every value is a
published value of another head, a range from the head maker's catalogue, or
a statement about a family of engines. The values the twin uses are in
[valve-seat-guide-case.json](../../twins/935-cylinder-head-cad-twin/params/valve-seat-guide-case.json),
each with its source id and hypothesis id (H16, H09).

## How it was read

Two search agents read public pages on 2026-11-22, about 40 fetches each, and
saved a raw copy of every page they took a value from. A host that refused
automated readers was left alone ([decision 0003](../decisions/0003-no-vendor-harvesting.md)).
The copies stay outside Git, in the private evidence folder of the twin; their
SHA-256 is under each source below. Before a value entered the repository
the main session read it a second time in the saved copy's visible text
(column *Second reading*). Quotes are short and exact; joined table cells are
shown with " | ".

How the twin uses them ([case](../../twins/935-cylinder-head-cad-twin/params/valve-seat-guide-case.json),
[record](../../twins/935-cylinder-head-cad-twin/params/valve-seat-guide.json)):

- **Valve heads 49 and 41.5 mm.** A 935 head of 2.85 to 3.3 litres falls in the
  maker's 2.0–2.9 or 3.0–3.5 litre family, and nothing published says which.
  49 and 41.5 mm are the only sizes common to both, and the production 930
  Turbo values. The ranges, 45–53 and 38–42.5 mm, are kept beside them.
- **8 mm stems**, as the maker lists for every family. The production 9 mm stem
  does not enter the fitted guide bore of 8.51 mm. Lengths from the
  published 8 mm-stem 911 valves: 109 and 108 mm.
- **Seat**: 45° with 30° and 75° cuts, contact widths 1.2 and 1.4 mm, the
  45° cone through the mid-band circle of the fitted seat. The insert bore is
  the fitted throat. The exhaust ring's outside diameter is read as the
  "46 MM" of the 930 Turbo listing; the inlet ring's (53.5 mm) is derived
  from it; the insert height (7 mm) is an explicit assumption.
- **Guides**: fitted outside diameters and stations on the scan (12.87 and
  13.02 mm against 13.06 mm published), 8.05 mm inside: the 8 mm stem plus
  an assumed 0.05 mm clearance.

Not found: the outside diameter, height and interference of any 911 or 930
seat ring (only the undefined "46 MM"); the installed height of a guide and
the clearance of a new stem; any cam of a 934, 935 or Xtreme head; any seat,
guide or valve length from the maker; any throat, bowl or guide-boss figure
of a 935 or Xtreme head. Hosts that would have helped refused automated
readers (below).

Contradictions: the 930 Turbo lift reads 9.7 / 8.9 mm on the homologation form
and 0.368 / 0.339 in (9.35 / 8.61 mm) in the cam maker's table, which states
its lifts at zero lash and a theoretical rocker ratio. One seller calls the
production inlet valve sodium-filled; no other source says so. Two sellers
disagree on whether guide suffix -50 is standard or first oversize.

other way. They make H09 a declared assignment, not a retired hypothesis:

## Hidden geometry (#697)

| Hypothesis | What was looked for | Found |
|---|---|---|
| H01 fin roots | fin pitch, thickness, root radius, gap floor of 911, 930, 935 or Xtreme heads | nothing |
| H04 port interiors | port shape, bowl, throat, short-side radius, guide boss, port volume, captioned cutaways | nothing published. The maker states it digitises and machines ports ([xtreme-ourshop](https://www.xtremecylinderheads.com/ourshop): "Used for digitizing ports and chambers"), so the geometry exists at the maker, unpublished |
| H07 galleries | oil or breather passages in an air-cooled 911-family head | a Porsche patent ([US 4941436 A](https://patents.google.com/patent/US4941436A/en)) routes oil through bores "perpendicularly to the joint face between the cylinder head and the camshaft housing" to a ring around the exhaust seat; a seller describes 935 heads with oil-cooled exhaust guides (2026-10-28 dossier, not re-read). Neither shows this head has a gallery |
| H08 cam-side protrusion | a 4 mm pin standing about 10 mm out of the cam-side face at about 36° | nothing that matches; the 993's head temperature sensor is "screwed into the cylinder head of the third cylinder" ([workshop-manuals](https://workshop-manuals.com/porsche/911_carrera_coupe/f6-3600cc_3.6l_sohc/powertrain_management/computers_and_control_systems/engine_temperature_sensor/component_information/locations/)), without a diameter or an angle |

A period article states that the factory 935 shared little with the 930
("just about the only parts it shared with the standard 930 were the
crankcase and crankshaft", [supercarnostalgia](https://supercarnostalgia.com/blog/porsche-935-76)),
while the maker offers its 3.0–3.5 litre head with a "930 or carrera intake
flange". Nothing published ties the maker's head to the factory 935's ports.

Where nothing was found the closure is kept and the request stays open. For
the ports only, the closure beyond the last scan section is replaced in the
port domain by the declared rule H17 (convex hull of the last scan section
and the fitted throat circle). The v2 body's inferred area is restated by
region in [inferred-share.json](../../twins/935-cylinder-head-cad-twin/params/inferred-share.json):
18.1% of the body, of which the port voids hold 2.2 points.

## Port flow (#700)

No flow figure of a 930, 934, 935 or Xtreme head could be opened: the maker
lists a flow bench ("Superflow SF 750 Flow Bench", xtreme-ourshop) and
publishes no numbers; the forum threads that quote figures refuse automated
readers. Published coefficients of other heads, kept for a later comparison
(each uses its own reference area, so they are not interchangeable):

| Source | Head | Value | Second reading |
|---|---|---|---|
| [NACA TN 701](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/api/citations/19930081426/downloads/19930081426.pdf) (1939) | air-cooled aircraft cylinder, 50° seat, faired port | "The average of the values for c was i.00" (inside-seat cone area; 0.88 on the outside-seat area), 0.1 in lift | re-read |
| [NACA TN 1035](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/api/citations/19930081967/downloads/19930081967.pdf) (1946) | in-line engine, two inlet valves | "The average flow coefficient for a Dressure drop of 2 inches of mercury is 0.322" (scan text as it reads) | re-read |
| [NACA TN 1365](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/api/citations/19930082002/downloads/19930082002.pdf) (1947) | single-cylinder test engine | best volumetric efficiency at an exhaust-to-inlet flow-capacity ratio of 0.69 (diameter ratio 0.83), exhaust and inlet at equal pressure | re-read |

The port CFD stopped at the audit, before any solver: see
[port-cfd.json](../../twins/935-cylinder-head-cad-twin/params/port-cfd.json).
