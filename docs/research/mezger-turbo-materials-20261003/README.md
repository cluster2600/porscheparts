# Mezger turbo engines: variants, materials and dimensions

Reviewed on 3 October 2026. This extends the [993 Turbo and K16 research](../993-turbo-20261002/README.md) for a 993 Turbo M64/60 project targeting more than 550 metric horsepower at the crankshaft. It adds **203 reviewed records linked to 46 sources**, including 41 newly catalogued sources. The machine-readable [reference](../../../catalog/reference/mezger-turbo-materials-20261003/README.md) and the separate [CPT/SFT preparation](../../../training/mezger-turbo-materials-20261003/README.md) preserve component, engine variant, source location and uncertainty.

“Mezger” identifies an engineering lineage, not one interchangeable engine. The air/oil-cooled 993 M64/60, mixed-cooling racing derivatives and water-cooled 996/997 Turbo/GT engines require separate component definitions. Naturally aspirated GT3 evidence is included as a named comparison. The direct-injected 997.2 Turbo and 991 GT3 are explicitly identified as later family boundaries.

## Turbo and racing variants

These are published configurations, not universal horsepower ceilings or a component interchangeability chart. Rounded litre values remain rounded; museum specifications identify their stated example. Original kW, PS, HP and hp wording is preserved in the register.

## Remaining documentary and measurement gaps

No source acquired in this increment defines the original M64/60 head, case, piston or cylinder's exact chemical grade and full metallurgical condition, or a local wall-thickness map. RR350 aftermarket heads, historical Alusil A390, generic supplier 4032 comments and GT3 titanium rods cannot fill those gaps.

A further 14 inspected Porsche articles are retained as [additional bibliographic readings](../../../catalog/reference/mezger-turbo-materials-20261003/additional-readings.json), without inventing extra component facts or training targets.

The next documentary targets and measurement distinctions are in [research-gaps.json](../../../catalog/reference/mezger-turbo-materials-20261003/research-gaps.json): model-specific Turbo Service Information Technik, Motorsport GT2/Evolution literature, homologation extensions, identified part drawings/material specifications and component metrology. FIA documents were not obtained because access returned 403. An external scan of a different racing head cannot identify hidden internal walls or alloy composition of the project engine.

Hans Mezger's [SAE 780718, Turbocharging Engines for Racing and Passenger Cars](https://saemobilus.sae.org/papers/turbocharging-engines-racing-passenger-cars-780718) is a useful primary-document lead: its publisher abstract and citation were read, its full text was not acquired. [SAE 830004](https://doi.org/10.4271/830004) discusses alloy 390 and earlier 911 experience, but its abstract does not identify the relevant 911 component. Both leads remain excluded from technical training.

For the greater-than-550-PS project, these records improve material identification and the list of measurements to obtain. They do not establish a unique original K16 map, the endurance of a hybrid turbo, or a qualified redesigned head. Existing geometry, evidence contracts, solver inputs and previously frozen training packs remain separate from this research increment.

## Data preparation and validation

The separate pack contains **200 CPT passages and 200 grounded SFT conversations**. Each format has 173 train, 18 validation and 9 test rows. Three records are excluded; five records explicitly teach the limits of the review. Application, component, original units, citations and caveats accompany every passage. Source-family and identical-reviewed-claim partitions respect the previous 993 pack. Pauter and independent Xtreme/Kline families are held out where compatible with those constraints.

These are authored factual summaries and supplied-record extraction examples. They are not copied manuals, an independent engineering benchmark, a tokenised corpus or a completed training run.
