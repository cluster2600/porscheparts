The conservative route preserves every original vertex coordinate and includes

not accuracy tolerances.

## Failures retained for the next operator

- Open3D's direct OBJ path merged four coincident coordinate entries and changed
  topology. Passing exact source arrays retained the original indexing; importer
  artifacts were separated from source defects.
- MeshLib import rounding changed coordinates slightly. Unchanged indexed values
  were restored only after checking indexing/connectivity, then inspected again.
- Initial MeshLib NumPy exports included vacant face slots as zero-index
  triangles. Applying `getValidFaces()` corrected the adapter; first-export
  counts were not treated as native-engine defects.
- PyMeshLab selected-face fills exceeded contour scope or left incomplete patches.
  Incomplete/intersecting patches and out-of-scope additions were rejected.
- The earlier contour-874 cap failed the stronger tangency screen and was excluded.
- Edge checks missed 48 disconnected used-vertex fans. Bounded removal of 46
  smaller-fan faces resolved them without moving source coordinates.
- A watertight voxel output still contained intersections and zero-area faces.
  CGAL cleanup and independent final checks were necessary.
