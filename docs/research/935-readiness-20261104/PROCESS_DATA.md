## Draft A — Falcontech qualification/data availability

Status: prepared locally only; no scan attached; no purchase authorisation.
Proposed role recipient: `info@falcontech.com.cn`, as published on the official
AM service page above.

Subject: Technical data availability — faithful Xtreme head replica qualification

Hello Falcontech engineering team,

We are evaluating a qualification route for an additively manufactured replica
of a scanned Xtreme air-cooled cylinder head. This is a data-availability enquiry,
not an order. The scan is not attached or authorised for transfer. Its digital
units are owner-confirmed millimetres; exact specimen revision, alloy and some
internal geometry remain unresolved. Please do not infer them from a catalogue.

Could you propose a technically suitable machine/material/process candidate and
state which of the following you can provide, under what licence/NDA, cost and
delivery terms? Please quote before any chargeable work. A proposed candidate
would remain subject to engineering acceptance and specimen-data closure.

1. Exact machine configuration, software/profile revisions, alloy specification,
   powder lot/chemistry/size distribution/reuse history, atmosphere, oxygen limits,
   substrate and preheat; identify the applicable qualified process window.
2. Versioned layer/exposure/contour/hatch/scan strategy, laser power/spot/speed,
   rotation, time-stamped vectors and dwell/recoat history, support/contact
   properties, build orientation and compensation. Please distinguish data that
   is exportable for independent simulation from operator-only access.
3. Temperature-dependent density, heat capacity, conductivity, expansion,
   elasticity, plasticity and any stress-relaxation model; phase-change data and
   optical/heat-source calibration where applicable. State orientation, powder
   versus solid assumptions, units, uncertainty and tested temperature ranges.
4. Heat treatment/HIP, support/base removal and machining sequence; oriented
   tensile, elevated-temperature, creep and fatigue data appropriate to intended
   head duty, with specimen history and statistical basis. No alloy substitution
   or equivalence to RR350 is assumed.
5. Calibration coupons with independently measured temperature/distortion, actual
   geometry, clamping/release sequence, porosity and CT/NDT, raw data and agreed
   validation tolerances. Separate calibration data from held-out validation.
6. A proposed qualification/inspection plan covering minimum walls, fin spacing,
   accessible machining and seats/threads, powder evacuation, cleanliness,
   leak/pressure testing and traceable final metrology. Flag inaccessible or
   unmanufacturable regions before a build offer.

Please distinguish existing public data, shareable customer data, unavailable
data and new paid tests. No redesign, build or scan disclosure is authorised by
this enquiry. Thank you.

## Draft B — BLT comparison enquiry

Status: prepared locally only; no scan attached; no purchase authorisation.
Proposed role recipient: `sales.global@xa-blt.com`, as published on the official
homepage above.

Subject: Technical data availability — metal cylinder-head process qualification

Hello BLT engineering team,

We are comparing qualification routes for a faithful replica of a scanned Xtreme
air-cooled cylinder head. Could your engineering team identify a candidate route
and itemise the data it can provide in these six categories?

1. Exact machine/configuration/software/profile, alloy, powder lot/chemistry/
   size/reuse, substrate, atmosphere, preheat and qualified process window.
2. Exportable versioned exposure/contour/hatch parameters, time-stamped scan
   vectors, layer/recoat/dwell history, support/contact properties, orientation
   and compensation; distinguish export rights from operator-only access.
3. Temperature-dependent density, heat capacity, conductivity, expansion,
   elasticity, plasticity, relaxation and heat-source/phase-change data, with
   orientation, temperature ranges, units, test methods and uncertainty.
4. Heat treatment/HIP, support/base removal and machining sequence; oriented
   hot tensile, creep and fatigue results with specimen history and statistics.
   No equivalence to the original casting or catalogue RR350 is presumed.
5. Calibration geometry, actual clamping/release, temperature/distortion and
   porosity/CT/NDT measurements, raw data and tolerances; separate calibration
   from held-out validation results.
6. A qualification/inspection plan for walls, fins, machining, seats/threads,
   powder evacuation, cleanliness, leaks/pressure and final metrology; identify
   inaccessible or unmanufacturable geometry before proposing a build.

No alloy or machine is selected, the scan is not attached or authorised for
transfer, and millimetre units do not establish the missing specimen metrology
or internal geometry. Please identify a candidate route and itemise public,
shareable, restricted, unavailable and chargeable data. Please quote before any
paid work. This enquiry would not authorise a build, redesign or order.
Please include the licence/NDA, cost and delivery terms for any data package.
Thank you. Both drafts remain local and unsent.
