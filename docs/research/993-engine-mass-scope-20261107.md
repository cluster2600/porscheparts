# 993 engine-mass scope: recovered community source

Issue #434 remains open. This is a documentary addendum to the historical
[carrier load-case notes](../../parts/993-eng-carrier-0001/evidence/load-cases.md),
not an accepted carrier load or replacement for the twin's engine-mass basis.

The [original post](https://forums.pelicanparts.com/3731741-post1.html), dated
January 27, 2008, is now registered as
[`SRC-PELICAN-TIMMINS-993-ENGINE-WEIGHTS`](../../catalog/sources/src-pelican-timmins-993-engine-weights.json).
The publisher attributes weighing to Steve Timmins using corner-balance scales.
Green chart cells denote reported weighings; blue component figures are estimates.

The 1995 Carrera row reads **331 lb** for engine and intake, and **412 lb** for
the configuration adding a light flywheel, heated headers and mufflers. A/C and
unspecified swap-related equipment are excluded. This is not a documented stock
complete-engine configuration. Fluid state, calibrated uncertainty and a full
accessory inventory remain unknown.

Using the exact conversion 1 lb = 0.45359237 kg gives 150.13907447 kg and
186.88005644 kg, respectively. The latter rounds to the existing 186.9 kg figure.
Its difference from 232 kg is 45.11994356 kg, approximately 45.1 kg. Conversion
digits do not establish measurement precision. Known configuration differences
prevent treating that gap as a like-for-like disagreement; they do not allocate
or explain all of it.

## Provenance and remaining gates

The [chart](https://forums.pelicanparts.com/uploads12/Motor%2Bweights1201484121.jpg)
was inspected independently of repository transcriptions. Its 19,489-byte JPEG
has SHA-256 `4edd0f6a3c6b4ef8d509aa9a5e7a0602b1e5607fe767ffcb11318838de21808a`.
The image remains private; no redistribution licence was found. The original
post is the publication of an attributed measurement, not independent proof of
instrument calibration or of every claimed inclusion.

The authorised workshop-manual p.15 check is still missing. The separately
registered Porsche booklet supports an engine figure excluding the gearbox, but
does not resolve fluid or accessory scope. No 1995 Carrera weighing is promoted
to a 993 Turbo or GT2 road-engine mass. The historical load-case file, declared
part masses, variant assignments and engineering gates remain unchanged.
