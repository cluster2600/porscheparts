# TI 1570 (02/00) — access and evidence of a distinct repair

Targeted search of September 7, 2026. The reference **1570 (02/00)** is confirmed
in the [official Porsche PET, plate 102-05](https://assets-v2.porsche.com/jp/-/media/Project/PCOM/SharedSite/PorscheClassic/Original-Parts-Catalogue/PDF-EN-WW/KAT013_E_911_94_KATALOG).
**The exact copy of this TI has not been found.** Exact English/German searches,
and searches restricted to the Porsche domain, returned PET references, not a
link to its text. No authentication failure was observed: we therefore cannot
claim that it sits behind an account or a subscription. No purchase, login or
unauthorized manual was used.

## Attributable Porsche bulletin, but a different reference

[Design911 hosts a scan of a Porsche Cars North America bulletin](https://www.design911.co.uk/uploads/pdfs/964_Cylinder_TSB.pdf):
**group 1, part identifier 1570, no. 9404, February 8, 1994**, concerning the
refacing of cylinder heads with tool 9526. All five pages were checked
visually, headers and figures included. It supersedes bulletin 9202; nothing
establishes that it is the same document as "02/00".

Scope p.1: Carrera 2/4 1989–1991, M64.01 up to **62M06836**, M64.02
Tiptronic up to **62M52757**. The repair uses the modified cylinders/pistons
and refaces the cylinder heads. The steel gasket **96410411520** sits in the
groove of the **cylinder** (p.1 and p.3), not in a cylinder-head groove inferred
from the drawing.

| Documentary transcription | Value / location |
|---|---|
| Axial refacing of the inner face marked 2 | **0.10 ± 0.02 mm**, p.4, figure 4 |
| Diameter of the seating surface after this refacing | **145 mm**, p.4, figure 4 |
| Maximum removal from the cylinder-head face | **0.20 mm**, p.4 |
| Stated surface finish | **32 micro-inches**, p.4; Ra parameter not explicitly named |
| Check before machining | Radial and lateral runout with a dial indicator, p.4 |
| Additional service reference | Carrera 2/4 manual, volume 1, **15-10e**, p.4 |

The text on p.4 explicitly designates the **machined surface**, then repeats the
diameter to be obtained on the cylinder-head–cylinder seating surface after
machining. The 145 mm dimension in figure 4 therefore designates **neither tool
9526 nor the lathe fixture**. The axial refacing is a removal from the existing
cylinder head, not a nominal material thickness to be created on a new head.

These transcriptions describe a **specific repair**, not a design drawing.
The 145 mm diameter is neither the engine bore nor a toleranced register
diameter, and is not adopted as a nominal M64 interface diameter.
No dimensioned groove width/depth, stud position or complete interface
tolerance is provided by these transcriptions. Applicability to the
993, M64.50/M64.60 and to our new turbo cylinder head is not established.
